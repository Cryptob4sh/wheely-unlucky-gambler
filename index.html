How it works

Only you see this, because you're logged in as admin. Viewers can look at everything but can't change anything.

1. Spinning a slip
  1. Pick the fixtures week with the buttons in Fixtures (e.g. 9–12 Oct). Only teams playing that week go on the teams ball.
  2. Tap Spin to spin the ball shown. It moves on to the next ball by itself: bet type, number of teams, the teams (one spin per team), then the stake.
  3. Spin the lot spins every ball in one go.
  4. To re-spin one ball, tap its row under the ball (Type of bet, Number of teams, The teams, Stake), then tap Spin.
  5. New slip clears the slip and starts again. Anyone watching sees every spin live on their own screen.
2. Filters: leagues, kick-off times and days
  • The Filters box sits under the four ball rows (only you see it). It shows how many teams are on the teams ball.
  • PL / CH / L1 / L2 / NL switch whole leagues on or off. NL is the UEFA Nations League (only on international weeks).
  • Early = before 3pm, 3pm = 3pm kick-offs, Late = after 3pm (5:30s, evening and Sunday 4:30s).
  • Fri / Sat / Sun / Mon switch days on or off. For a Saturday 3pm acca, leave only Sat and 3pm on.
  • Games that don't match are faded in the Fixtures list. At least one chip in each group must stay on.
  • Once a team is picked, its opponent comes off the ball, so you never get both sides of the same game.
3. The £5 weekly budget
  • The stake ball only offers what's left of this week's budget (Monday to Sunday). Spin £2 and the next slip's stake ball only has up to £3.
  • The betting slip shows "This week: £X of £5 left". Winnings don't top it up.
  • To change the £5 or the stake options, tap the ✏️ on the Stake row, edit, and tap Save. Leave the budget blank for no limit.
4. Odds, saving and results
  • When the slip is complete, type the bookie's odds in Odds on the betting slip (e.g. 22/1, 9/2, evens or 23.0). It shows To return £X.
  • Tap Save to results. Saved slips appear under Results, grouped by week.
  • Teams tick themselves ✓ or ✗ once final scores come in, with the score shown. Auto ticks have a dashed border. Tap a tick to override it: won, lost, then back to automatic.
  • Winning slips count the odds' return in profit/loss automatically. If the bookie paid a different amount (cash-out, boost), type it in Paid out £.
  • The red × on a saved slip deletes it after you confirm. Reset all wipes every slip, vote and the season totals (it asks first). Download CSV saves all results as a spreadsheet.
5. Bookie's slip
  • On a saved slip, tap Add bookie slip and choose a screenshot of the bet from your bookie app.
  • It shows as a thumbnail next to the slip. Anyone can tap it to see it full size. Remove takes it off.
6. Followers: live spins, predictions, sharing
  • Viewers watch your spins live and see the slip build up as you go.
  • When a slip is complete they can pick a nickname and vote Lands or Busts until the first kick-off. Saved slips show the votes and who called it, and the Followers' leaderboard ranks the best predictors.
  • Share image on the betting slip (or ⇪ on a saved slip) makes a branded picture with your logo for WhatsApp, TikTok or Instagram. Copy slip copies it as text.
7. Fixtures and scores updating
  • Fixtures and final scores live in your GitHub (Cryptob4sh/wheely-unlucky-gambler, file fixtures.json) and the site reads them from there. Claude updates them on a schedule, so there's nothing to do.
  • If a week's fixtures look wrong or missing, ask Claude to "update the Wheely Unlucky fixtures".
  • Netlify's free plan has a monthly allowance and every republish uses some of it, so only republish when the app itself changes. Fixture and score updates don't need a republish.
8. Logging in and the phone app
  • Admin login uses your Firebase password. Your phone stays logged in until you tap Log out. Logged out, you see exactly what followers see.
  • To add it to your home screen: iPhone, open it in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. Android, Chrome menu → Install app.
